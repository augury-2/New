## 5. Internal Inconsistencies Corrected

1. **Missing subsection 5.2** — the manuscript jumped from 5.1 to 5.3; the heading "5.2 Measurement model assessment" was restored.
2. **Section 3.2 unnumbered** — the SDT heading relied on automatic list numbering; explicit numbering applied.
3. **5.4 titled "Structural Model and Hypothesis Evaluation"** — no structural model is estimated (the analysis is configurational), so the heading is now "5.4 Hypothesis evaluation".
4. **Proposition P1 never resolved** — 3.3 stated P1 but no section evaluated it; 5.4 now states that three sufficient configurations rather than one necessary condition support P1.
5. **UX × UE coverage reported inconsistently** — the original Table 3 gave raw coverage 0.219 and unique coverage 0.046 while Table 5 gave 0.218 and 0.047. Consolidating into one table removes the discrepancy; the values from the subset/superset and intermediate-solution tables (0.219 and 0.046) are reported.
6. **Coverage percentages** — the text reported "20.84%", "24.15%" and "21.90%" against tabulated values of 0.208, 0.241 and 0.219; these restatements were removed, so only the tabulated values remain.
7. **Metaverse-usage frequency** — the text stated that "60.58% used the metaverse frequently (several times a week)", but Table 1 reports 16.67% daily and 31.41% several times a week (48.08% combined; 71.46% at least weekly). The 60.58% figure corresponds to no combination of the tabulated rows, so the sentence was removed and Table 1 now carries the frequency distribution. **Please confirm whether 60.58% refers to something else.**
8. **Figure 1 was never cited** in the text; it is now cited in 2.2.
9. **Spelling** mixed British and American forms (favourable/favorable, standardised/standardized, utilising/utilizing); standardised to British throughout the body text. Reference titles were left exactly as published.
10. **"FsQCA" / "fsQCA" / "Fuzzy set" / "Fuzzy-set"** were used interchangeably; standardised to "fsQCA" and "fuzzy-set qualitative comparative analysis".
11. **Measurement table header banner** read "Factor Loadings" above columns that also report alpha, AVE, SE, Z and p; changed to "Measurement properties".
12. **Income percentages in Table 1 do not match their counts** (see item 7 in section 6). The values were left exactly as reported.

## 6. Items Requiring Author Attention (not changed)

These are substantive matters that an editor cannot resolve without the authors:

1. **Table 1 income percentages contradict the counts.** With N = 312: 57 respondents is 18.27%, not 18.40%, and 75 respondents is 24.04%, not 23.91% (the other two bands are correct, and the four values were evidently forced to sum to 100). Gender is also rounded from 45.83%/54.17% to 46.00%/54.00%. Nothing was altered; please correct at source.
2. **The editor asked for the demographic table to be removed.** It has been retained on instruction, with the duplicating narrative cut instead. If the editor objects again, converting it back to prose frees 280 words.
3. **AVE below 0.50** for three constructs (UE 0.481, UX 0.481, BSUC 0.448). Reviewers will raise convergent validity. Consider reporting composite reliability alongside AVE, or justifying the values.
4. **Csikszentmihalyi (1990)** is referenced to *Journal of Leisure Research*, 24(1), 93–94, which is a two-page review of the book rather than the book itself. The book reference should replace it.
5. **Jia et al. (2022)** is listed as *MIS Quarterly*, 45(3), 1603–1634 with a 2022 date; volume and year should be verified.
6. **No common-method-bias test** is reported. Reviewers of single-informant survey work usually expect one; Podsakoff et al. (2003) was removed from the list because it was never cited.
7. **Reference title case** is mixed (some titles in Title Case, others in sentence case). Automated conversion risked damaging proper nouns (Metaverse, NFT, AR/VR), so a manual pass is recommended.
8. **Figures must be supplied as separate high-resolution files** at submission, per Emerald policy, with the in-text captions retained as placement markers.
