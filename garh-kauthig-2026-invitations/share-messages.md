Copy the block, paste into the group, attach the matching invitation from
`output/images/jpg-share/` (1400 px, ~400 KB — sized for messaging).

Formatting uses WhatsApp's own markup: `*bold*`, `_italic_`. Paste it raw; the
asterisks and underscores turn into formatting when sent.

## 1. Faculty group

```
*GARH KAUTHIG 2026*
_Tradition, Togetherness, and Timeless Culture_

Respected Sir / Ma'am,

The Graphic Era School of Management, together with Swaragini — The Cultural
Society of Graphic Era University, cordially invites you to *Garh Kauthig 2026*,
an afternoon of Garhwali music, dance and celebration, held as part of the
EMERGE Induction Program 2026.

*Date:* Thursday, 13 August 2026
*Time:* 01:00 PM onwards
*Venue:* Silver Jubilee Convention Centre, Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)

You know our students from the lecture hall. This is quite another view of them —
in traditional attire, drumming the dhol and damau, singing the songs their
grandmothers sang. Your presence would mean a great deal to every one of them.

The invitation is attached. We look forward to welcoming you.

_Graphic Era School of Management · Swaragini_
```

## 2. Research Scholars group

```
*GARH KAUTHIG 2026*
_Tradition, Togetherness, and Timeless Culture_

Dear Research Scholars,

Much of what a scholar chases through an archive, a _kauthig_ keeps alive in a
courtyard. On 13 August, the Silver Jubilee Convention Centre becomes exactly
that courtyard — and you are warmly invited.

*Date:* Thursday, 13 August 2026
*Time:* 01:00 PM onwards
*Venue:* Silver Jubilee Convention Centre, Graphic Era (Deemed to be University)

The dhol and damau, the hudka, the ransingha, the jhora circle that closes only
when the last dancer joins it — not as exhibits, but as a living tradition,
performed by students who learned them at home rather than from a reading list.

Come for the scholarship of it, or simply for the afternoon. Invitation attached.

_Graphic Era School of Management · Swaragini_
```

## 3. MBA 2025–27 Seniors group

```
*GARH KAUTHIG 2026*
_Tradition, Togetherness, and Timeless Culture_

Seniors —

This time last year, somebody else was holding the door open for you.
On 13 August, it's your turn.

*Date:* Thursday, 13 August 2026
*Time:* 01:00 PM onwards
*Venue:* Silver Jubilee Convention Centre

Take the stage. Teach a step to someone who has never danced it. Cheer loudest
for the fresher whose hands are shaking before their first performance — a year
ago that was you, and somebody cheered.

Come in your finest, come early, and come ready to make some noise. The hills
always sound better when there are more of us singing.

Invitation attached. See you there.

_Graphic Era School of Management · Swaragini_
```

## 4. MBA 2026–28 Freshers group

```
*GARH KAUTHIG 2026*
_Tradition, Togetherness, and Timeless Culture_

Welcome, everyone!

You have barely unpacked, and already there is a festival.

*Date:* Thursday, 13 August 2026
*Time:* 01:00 PM onwards
*Venue:* Silver Jubilee Convention Centre

Some of you grew up with the dhol and damau. Some of you will hear them properly
for the first time. By the end of the afternoon it will be hard to tell which of
you was which — and that, more or less, is the whole point of a _kauthig_.

So wear something bright, learn a step, and sing the chorus even if you don't
know the words yet. Nobody does at first.

This is your first celebration as one of us. We're very glad you're here.

Invitation attached. See you on the 13th!

_Graphic Era School of Management · Swaragini_
```

## 5. Covering note for the three official invitations

The Vice Chancellor's, Pro Vice Chancellor's and Registrar's letters are meant to
go formally — printed and delivered, or attached to an official email — rather
than posted in a group. If a covering email is needed:

```

Respected Sir,

Please find attached an invitation to *Garh Kauthig 2026*, a celebration of
Garhwali heritage presented by the Graphic Era School of Management together
with Swaragini — The Cultural Society of Graphic Era University, as part of the
EMERGE Induction Program 2026.

The programme begins at 01:00 PM on Thursday, 13 August 2026, at the Silver
Jubilee Convention Centre.

It would be our privilege to have you grace the occasion with your esteemed
presence.

With warm regards,
Graphic Era School of Management · Swaragini
```

### Notes

- These carry only what the poster states. There is no dress code on the poster,
  so the messages *suggest* traditional attire rather than announcing a code —
  change the wording if a code is set.
- No contact number is given anywhere, since the poster carries none. Add one to
  the end of each message if you would like queries directed somewhere.
- The messages are deliberately emoji-free so they read the same on every device
  and suit the faculty and scholars groups. If you would like a version with
  emoji for the two student groups, that is a quick change.
